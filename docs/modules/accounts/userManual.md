# Accounts User Manual

## Overview
The Accounts module provides a comprehensive view of your business's financial health. It tracks all income, expenses, assets, and liabilities through a system of double-entry vouchers.

## 1. Financial Reports
Access these reports from the **Dashboard > Accounts** menu.

### Trial Balance
*   **Purpose**: A list of all accounts and their current balances.
*   **Use Case**: Quick verification that total Debits equal total Credits.
*   **Features**: Filter by date to see the state of accounts at any specific point in time.

### Profit & Loss (Income Statement)
*   **Purpose**: Shows profitability over a period.
*   **Structure**:
    *   **Revenue**: Sales and other income.
    *   **Expenses**: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), operating expenses.
    *   **Net Income**: The bottom line (Revenue - Expenses).
*   **Actions**: Select a date range (e.g., "This Month", "Last Quarter") to analyze performance.

### Balance Sheet
*   **Purpose**: A snapshot of financial position.
*   **Equation**: `Assets = Liabilities + Equity`.
*   **Key Sections**:
    *   **Assets**: Cash, Bank, Inventory, Accounts Receivable.
    *   **Liabilities**: Accounts Payable, Loans, Taxes Owed.
    *   **Equity**: Owner's Capital, Retained Earnings.

## 2. Ledgers & Sub-Ledgers

### General Ledger
*   View the detailed transaction history for *any* specific account (e.g., "Cash in Hand", "Sales Revenue").
*   Shows the running balance for that account.

### Accounts Receivable (Customer Balances)
*   Shows how much money is owed to you by customers.
*   **Aging**: View outstanding balances by age (0-30 days, 30-60 days, etc.) to identify overdue payments.

### Accounts Payable (Supplier Balances)
*   Shows how much money you owe to suppliers.
*   Helps manage cash flow by tracking upcoming payment obligations.

## 3. Vouchers (Transactions)
A **Voucher** is the primary record of a transaction.

*   **Draft Vouchers**: Vouchers that are created but not yet finalized. They do not affect reports.
*   **Posted Vouchers**: Finalized transactions. Once posted, the values update all financial reports and cannot be deleted (only reversed).

### Types of Vouchers
1.  **Payment**: Money leaving the business (paying expenses, suppliers).
2.  **Receipt**: Money entering the business (customer payments).
3.  **Journal**: General adjustments (depreciation, corrections).
4.  **Contra**: Transfers between Cash/Bank accounts.
5.  **Sales/Purchase**: Automatically created from the Sales and Purchase modules.
