# Accounts Module Documentation

## 1. Overview
The Accounts Module implements a complete **Double-Entry Bookkeeping System**. It handles all financial transactions, producing the core financial statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss).

## 2. Core Architecture

### 2.1 Chart of Accounts (CoA)
The backbone of the system.
*   **Types**: `ASSET`, `LIABILITY`, `EQUITY`, `REVENUE`, `EXPENSE`.
*   **Structure**: Hierarchical (Parent/Child accounts).
*   **Active/Inactive**: Accounts can be deactivated but not deleted if they have transaction history.

### 2.2 Vouchers (The Transaction Layer)
Every financial change is a "Voucher".
*   **Voucher Types**:
    *   `JOURNAL`: Manual adjustments (e.g., depreciation).
    *   `PAYMENT`: Cash/Bank OUT (Paying suppliers, expenses).
    *   `RECEIPT`: Cash/Bank IN (Customer payments).
    *   `CONTRA`: Cash <-> Bank transfers.
    *   `PURCHASE`: Automated from Purchase Module.
    *   `SALES`: Automated from Sales Module.
*   **Structure**: A Voucher contains multiple **Lines**.
    *   Sum of Debit Lines MUST EQUAL Sum of Credit Lines.

## 3. Automated Integrations

### 3.1 Purchase Integration
*   **On Receive**:
    *   **Dr** Inventory Account (Asset)
    *   **Cr** Accounts Payable (Liability)
*   **On Payment**:
    *   **Dr** Accounts Payable (Liability)
    *   **Cr** Cash/Bank (Asset)

### 3.2 Sales Integration
*   **On Delivery**:
    *   **Dr** Cost of Goods Sold (Expense)
    *   **Cr** Inventory (Asset)
*   **On Invoice**:
    *   **Dr** Accounts Receivable (Asset)
    *   **Cr** Sales Revenue (Revenue)

### 3.3 Production Integration
*   **Start**:
    *   **Dr** WIP (Asset)
    *   **Cr** Raw Material Inventory (Asset)
*   **Complete**:
    *   **Dr** Finished Goods Inventory (Asset)
    *   **Cr** WIP (Asset)

## 4. Reports Engine
Reports are generated by aggregating voucher lines based on account types.

### 4.1 Balance Sheet
*   **Equation**: Assets = Liabilities + Equity
*   **Timeframe**: As of a specific date (cumulative).

### 4.2 Profit & Loss (Income Statement)
*   **Equation**: Net Profit = Revenue - Expenses
*   **Timeframe**: Over a period (e.g., this month, this year).

### 4.3 Trial Balance
*   Lists closing balances of ALL accounts to ensure Debits = Credits.

## 5. Security & Audit
*   **System Actions**: Vouchers created automatically (Purchase/Production) are flagged as `isSystemAction` and cannot be easily edited manually to preserve integrity.
*   **User Logs**: All creates/updates/deletes are logged with User ID and timestamp.
