# Accounts Module - User Manual

## Introduction
The Accounts Module handles all the money-related aspects of your business. It runs in the background for most operations (sales, purchases) but allows full manual control when needed.

## 1. Chart of Accounts (CoA)
This is your list of "buckets" where money is tracked.
1.  Navigate to **Accounts > Chart of Accounts**.
2.  You will see a tree of accounts (Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expense).
3.  **Creating an Account**:
    *   Click **Add Account**.
    *   **Name**: E.g., "Office Expenses".
    *   **Type**: Select correct category (e.g., Expense).
    *   **Parent**: Group it under a main account (e.g., "Operating Expenses").

## 2. Vouchers (Transactions)
Use Vouchers to record manual transactions not handled by Sales/Purchase/Production.

### Recording an Expense (Payment Voucher)
1.  Navigate to **Accounts > Payments**.
2.  Click **New Payment**.
3.  **Paid From**: Select your Cash or Bank account.
4.  **Paid To**: Select the Expense account (e.g., "Electricity Bill").
5.  **Amount**: Enter the value.
6.  **Narrative**: Add a note.
7.  Click **Save**.

### Contra Entry (Bank Deposit/Withdrawal)
1.  Navigate to **Accounts > Contra**.
2.  Click **New Contra**.
3.  **Source**: "Cash in Hand" (if depositing).
4.  **Destination**: "Bank Account".
5.  **Amount**: Enter value.
6.  Click **Save**.

## 3. Financial Reports
To see how your business is doing:

### Balance Sheet
Shows your financial position today.
1.  Navigate to **Accounts > Reports > Balance Sheet**.
2.  Check **Total Assets** vs **Total Liabilities**.

### Profit & Loss Statement
Shows your performance over time.
1.  Navigate to **Accounts > Reports > Profit & Loss**.
2.  Select the **Date Range** (e.g., This Month).
3.  Review **Total Revenue** - **Total Expenses** = **Net Profit**.

### Trial Balance
A technical report for accountants to verify accuracy.
1.  Navigate to **Accounts > Reports > Trial Balance**.
2.  Ensure Debit Total equals Credit Total.
